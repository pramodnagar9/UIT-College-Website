About NCC

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) is a youth development organisation that promotes discipline, leadership and teamwork among students.

Objectives of NCC

  • Develop leadership qualities
  • Build discipline and confidence
  • Encourage teamwork and unity
  • Promote social responsibility
  • Develop a spirit of adventure

NCC Activities

  • Parades and drills
  • Community service
  • Awareness programmes
  • National celebrations
  • Training camps
