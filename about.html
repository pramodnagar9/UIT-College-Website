About Our Institute

University Institute of Technology (UIT) is an institute of Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal.

The institute focuses on technical education, engineering, innovation and research.

Our Vision

To develop skilled engineers and responsible professionals through quality technical education.

Our Mission

  • Quality technical education
  • Research and innovation
  • Practical learning
  • Student development
